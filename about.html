WHO WE ARE

Making Journey Planning Easier

Know Your Transport Fare is a web-based project designed to help people estimate transport fares before travelling between Nigerian cities.

The platform allows users to select their departure city, destination, transport type and number of passengers to calculate an illustrative fare estimate.

Users can also explore popular routes and save their journeys in their browser for future reference.

NEED HELP?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

What is Know Your Transport Fare?

It is a web-based project that helps users estimate transport fares between selected Nigerian cities.

Are the displayed fares real-time prices?

No. The fares are illustrative sample estimates used for demonstration. They are not connected to live transport operators or booking services.

Can I save my calculated journeys?

Yes. You can save your journeys, and the website stores them in your browser using Local Storage.

Can I book a bus or pay for a ticket here?

No. This project provides fare estimation only. It does not support ticket booking, seat reservations or payments.

Does the website work on mobile phones?

Yes. The website is designed to adapt to mobile phones, tablets and computers.
